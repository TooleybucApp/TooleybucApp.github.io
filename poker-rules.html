Tooleybuc Sporting Club – Poker Rules

Texas Hold'em · weekly games and tournaments

The Club follows the 2018 National Poker League rules as its guideline for weekly games and tournaments. The Tournament Director may override these rules where it suits a small club tournament such as Tooleybuc Sporting Club.

1. Tournament Director

The Tournament Directors are John Cameron and/or Club staff. Floor people and Tournament Directors put the best interest of the game and fairness first. Unusual circumstances can mean fairness takes priority over the technical rules. The Tournament Director's decision is final, and the Tournament Director's penalties override National Poker League guidelines.

2. Phones

Players may not talk on the phone at the poker table or use a phone while in a hand. If a player slows the game by using a phone, the Tournament Director may issue penalties.

3. Etiquette

Good manners and courtesy towards other players are essential. No insulting or offensive comments to or about other players. Threatening or intimidating behaviour will not be accepted; the three-stage penalty system applies, and the Tournament Director may exclude a player without warning if their behaviour warrants it.

The following are improper and can lead to a warning, penalty, suspension or ban:

  1. Abusive or disruptive behaviour.
  2. Cheating.
  3. Deliberately acting out of turn.
  4. Agreeing to check a hand out when a third player is all-in.
  5. Soft playing – refusing to bet against a certain opponent when heads-up.
  6. Chip-dumping.
  7. Reading a hand for another player at the showdown before it is placed face up on the table.
  8. Revealing the contents of a live hand before betting is complete.
  9. Revealing the contents of a folded hand before betting is complete – not even to someone out of the pot.
  10. Needlessly stalling the game.
  11. Deliberately discarding hands away from the muck.
  12. Stacking chips in a way that gets in the way of dealing or viewing cards.
  13. Messy chip stacks – all players must have a clear view of larger-value chips (you cannot hide your chips).
  14. Making statements or taking action that could unfairly influence play, whether or not you are in the pot.
  15. Using a phone for communication.
  16. Rabbit hunting or window shopping.
  17. Burn cards are part of the board and must stay visible with the board at all times.

4. Privacy

A copy of the full poker rules and the Club's Privacy Policy is available on the day from the Tournament Director.
