Local rules & course info
  1. Out of bounds: all ground outside the Club's boundary fences, including the car park and Motel.
  2. Immovable obstructions (Rule 24-2): (a) all trees or shrubs under two club lengths; (b) all protective fences; (c) all irrigation equipment.
  3. Water hazards: all dams and the river, defined by red markers.
  4. A ball lying in casual water may be lifted and dropped, not nearer the hole.
  5. A ball striking power lines must be played again without penalty.
  6. Wheel marks, paths, roads and worked-up soil may be treated as ground under repair (G.U.R.).
  7. Relief may be taken from cracks through the green without penalty.
  • The lowest marker is captain of each group and is responsible for speed, rules and etiquette.
  • 150 metre markers are measured from the centre of the fairway to the centre of the green.
  • Please book a tee online and pay before you play (online or cash). Clubhouse opens 11am.
  • Take a bucket of sand and repair divots on the fairway and ball marks on the greens.
